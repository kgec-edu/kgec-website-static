📍 Campus Location & Transit Guide

Official Address: Kalyani Government Engineering College, Kalyani, Nadia District, West Bengal — 741235, India.

🚆 By Train
Kalyani Railway Station (Sealdah–Ranaghat Line) is located 3.5 km away. Frequent local trains run from Sealdah (Kolkata). E-rickshaws and autos are readily available directly to the campus gate.
🚗 By Road
Directly connected to Kolkata via the 6-lane Kalyani Expressway (approx. 50 km) and National Highway 12 (NH-12). Direct bus services connect Kalyani to Kolkata, Barasat, and Siliguri.
✈️ By Air
Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport (CCU), Kolkata is located approximately 48 km from the college campus, accessible via taxi or the expressway in ~75 minutes.
